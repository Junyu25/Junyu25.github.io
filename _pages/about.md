👋 Hi, I'm **Junyu Chen** — a biohacker from China working at the intersection of biology and computation.

My background is in **bioinformatics and the microbiome**: I build reproducible pipelines for metagenomics and whole-genome sequencing, analyze virulence-factor and CAZy gene abundance, and mine biosynthetic gene clusters (BGCs) from microbial communities. I earned my B.Eng. in Bioengineering from Shenzhen University and worked as a research assistant at the Shenzhen Institute of Synthetic Biology (Chinese Academy of Sciences).

🔭 Currently interested in the microbiome, metagenomics, and tools that make computational biology reproducible.

On this site you'll find my [CV](/cv/), [publications](/publications/), and a [blog](/blog/) where I write — in both English and Chinese — about bioinformatics, learning systems, and side projects.
